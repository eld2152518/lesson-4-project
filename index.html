About Equipment Safety

What Is Airsoft?

Airsoft is a sport where players use fake guns that copies real guns that fires lightweight plastic BBs.

The goal of a game can vary, but players work together to complete objectives and have fun.

Airsoft Equipment

Players commonly use eye protection and other equipment designed for the sport.

Airsoft replicas come in many different styles. Some players enjoy different historical or fictional designs.

The equipment should be used responsibly and according to the rules.

Always follow the rules and safety requirements of the airsoft field you are playing at.

Safety First

Safety is one of the most important parts of airsoft. Players should always use eye protection.

Never remove your eye protection during a game.
